            Rider Switch

            For when a kid is too short for a ride or doesn't want to go. Tell the Cast Member at the entrance, with everyone there. Group A rides while group B waits outside. Then group B rides without waiting in line again.

            Leave the line and come back

            Attraction Queue Re-Entry: a kid who needs out can leave while the rest of us hold our place, then rejoin before boarding. Or they wait outside and meet us at the front. Tell the Cast Member at the front you're using Re-Entry or Meet-Up. No reason needed.

            Getting DAS return times in the park

            In the app: follow the steps below from anywhere in the park.

            In person: a Cast Member can book return times at Guest Relations in City Hall#1 (stop GR on our map, on your left as you enter Main Street) or at any Guest Experience Team location around the park. This helps if a phone dies.

            Rules to know: your party can hold one DAS return time at a time. After you use it, you can request the next one 10 minutes later. You can hold a virtual queue spot at the same time.

            On our route: while you ride the shorter-wait stops, hold a DAS return for the next long one. Good DAS picks are TRON#68, Jungle Cruise#15, Tiana's#25, Big Thunder#26, Peter Pan#43, Seven Dwarfs#48 and Space Mountain#70.

            Source: Walt Disney World's Disability Access Service page, checked Oct 8, 2026.

            Book a DAS return time

            Follow these steps to book return times.

            1. Log in to your account on the My Disney Experience app
            2. Tap the [≡] icon on the bottom right-hand corner of the screen
            3. On the next screen, select the Disability Access Service (DAS) button
            4. Choose the Attraction you'd like to visit
            5. On the following page, select Request DAS Return Time
            6. Next, select who will be joining the Guest with the DAS accommodation and press Continue
            7. Review the return time, height requirements and party members then press Confirm
            8. On the next screen, you will see a confirmation message. Now tap on View My Day
            9. The attraction name and return time will appear on the My Day page of the My Disney Experience app

            Once you’ve completed this, your return time will appear on the My Day screen of the My Disney Experience app.

            Cancelation

            If you click on the return time tile on the My Day page of the My Disney Experience app, you’ll see an option to cancel. From there, you can cancel the return time or remove party members who no longer wish to experience the attraction. Please note that removing the Guest with the DAS accommodation will automatically cancel the return time.

            If someone gets lost

            • Kids: stop and find a Cast Member, anyone in a Disney name tag. Show them your name tag.
            • Grown-ups: tell the nearest Cast Member. Show this morning's photo.
            • Meeting spot: front of City Hall#1 on Main Street (stop GR).
            • A lost child who isn't found right away is walked to the park's lost-persons spot. Cast Members will know where.
